## 🎬 How to Present in Canva

1. Create a new presentation in Canva: **1920 × 1080 px (16:9)**.
2. Paste each slide's prompt into **Canva Magic Design** or layout the cards matching `docs/presentation/nagarik_pitch_forge.html`.
3. Set page transitions to **Match Move (Smart Animate)** or **Rise (0.6s)** for cinematic 3D flow.
4. Click **Present > Presenter View** in Canva to see your speaker notes while advancing slides with your keyboard or remote clicker!
