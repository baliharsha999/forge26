THE BROKEN DISCOVERY JOURNEY

Why Millions Fall Through The Bureaucratic Maze

Statutory welfare discovery is the first critical gate. When discovery fails, life-changing benefits expire unclaimed.

STEP 1
Ask Around
Inquire with relatives, ward leaders, or local CSC operators. Mixed accuracy.
STEP 2
Search Portals
Hit multiple disconnected ministry, state, and district websites.
STEP 3
Repeat Visits
Repeated physical office trips to uncover hidden document criteria.
FINAL FAILURE
Give Up or Pay
Abandon statutory entitlements or pay intermediaries to file basic forms.

BUILT & VERIFIED CAPABILITIES

Six Practical Tools, Zero Guesswork

Every capability is implemented in our extension and backend codebase. No buzzwords, no dummy promises.

Voice in Mother Tongue
Continuous speech recognition in Hindi, Telugu, and English, converting plain spoken words into a structured petition.
15 Verified Schemes
Curated repository with verbatim official gazette texts, official URLs, and 2026-10-30 verification timestamps.
Eligibility Matcher
Transparent criteria evaluator that explains exactly why a citizen qualifies across caste, age, income, and landholding.
Safe In-Page Auto-Fill
Portal adapters inject values into CPGRAMS & municipal forms. Never auto-submits; citizen handles OTP/CAPTCHA.
3-Outcome Claim Verifier
Classifies viral scheme claims into: "Matches official source", "Contradicts official source", or "Couldn't verify".
Statutory Escalations
Auto-drafts formal Reminders, First Appeals under Public Services Guarantee Acts, and Section 6(1) RTI petitions when SLA lapses.
