## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, deeply dignified civic interface for public welfare discovery, rights navigation, and algorithmic entitlement matching. Grounded in the symbol of the lit diya (the lamp of knowledge and empowerment from the brand emblem), the system bridges computational civic intelligence with profound cultural respect and absolute clarity.

The target audience spans diverse demographics, from tech-literate grassroots advocates to first-generation digital citizens and low-literacy users navigating crucial government welfare schemes (housing, pensions, agrarian support, healthcare subsidies). The emotional response must evoke institutional integrity, protective calm, non-bureaucratic warmth, and absolute trust. 

The aesthetic is **Precision Sovereign Dark**: deep obsidian and charcoal structural planes layered with refined champagne gold, brushed platinum, and disciplined terracotta/deep saffron accents drawn straight from the emblem's core flame. It rejects ephemeral SaaS gradients and eliminates frivolous decorum; every element possesses tactile clarity, ultra-high contrast, and immediate legibility.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high luminance contrast on deep architectural dark grounds, deliberately avoiding purples and greens in favor of sovereign metallics, solar ochres, and cool structural navies.

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / Metallic Warm Gold):** The beacon of qualification and primary actions. Echoes illumination and dignity. Supported by lighter champagne tones (`#F6E7B0`) for fine linear highlights and metallic sheen.
- **Secondary (`#E07A42` / Saffron Terracotta):** Direct chromatic lineage from the central Diya flame in the logo. Used for urgent citizenship calls, high-priority notifications, state-benefit flags, and warm focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#CBD5E1` / Refined Platinum & Brushed Steel):** Structural demarcation, subtle outlines, passive counters, and auxiliary metadata. Provides clean industrial precision without visual noise.
- **Neutral Core (`#121417` / Deep Obsidian Charcoal):** The bedrock background canvas. Supported by layered dark tiers: `#0A0C0E` (abyssal canvas), `#1A1E24` (elevated surface tier 1), and `#242932` (surface tier 2), with midnight navy undertones (`#151B28`) in structural hero cards honoring the navy linework of the logo emblem.

### Color Rules & Accessibility
- **No Purple, No Green:** Success states, verified entitlements, and status badges rely on luminous platinum-gilded checks, pure warm gold highlights, or crisp sovereign navy badges with high-contrast monochrome iconography rather than traditional green/purple indicators.
- **Contrast Ratios:** Text meets strict WCAG AAA standards against charcoal foundations (minimum 7:1 for body and 4.5:1 for oversized interactive headers).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes supreme cognitive accessibility and immediate comprehension for low-literacy or first-time digital citizens.

- **Primary Voice (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for wide aperture openings, humanist curves, tall x-height, and balanced optical weights that prevent letter blurring on budget mobile LCD displays. It handles both multilingual vernacular transcripts and clean English headlines with zero fatigue.
- **Civic Data Voice (`Space Grotesk`):** Reserved for technical tokens, entitlement percentages, scheme registration numbers, monetary allocations (e.g., ₹ amounts), step meters, and system badges. Its geometric, monospaced-adjacent cadence conveys algorithmic certainty and computational governance.

All critical scheme criteria are paired with plain-language summaries set in `body-lg` at 18px to assist reading comfort across diverse vision abilities.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt structural rhythm within a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop and tablet, converting to an un-cramped 4-column flow on mobile.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width 1280px container, centered. Margin is `2.5rem`, column gutter is `1.5rem`. Enables persistent dual-pane workflows (e.g., Citizen Eligibility Dossier on the left; AI Scheme Discovery Stream on the right).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Margin is `2rem`, gutter is `1.25rem`. Complex matrices reflow into vertically stacked cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Margin is `1rem`, gutter is `1rem`. Touch targets expand to a minimum height of 48px to accommodate tremor and non-stylus direct finger navigation. Bottom sheets replace modal dialogues to preserve one-handed usability.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft colored drop shadows or blurry neomorphic extrusions. Instead, depth is conveyed through **metallic edge luminescence, brushed steel precision borders, and stacked obsidian planes**.

1. **Base Plane (`#0A0C0E`):** Background viewport. Completely matte to ground the experience and optimize battery conservation on OLED screens.
2. **Elevated Containers (`#16191E` to `#1D222A`):** Interactive cards, inquiry containers, and scheme modules. Given subtle 1px brushed steel borders (`rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.12)`).
3. **Active & Entitled Containers:** Cards highlighting qualified schemes receive a dual-gradient border transition: starting with warm metallic gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`) fading into midnight navy (`rgba(21, 27, 40, 0.8)`).
4. **Modals & Sticky Overlays:** Surface color `#1A1F26` with backdrop blur of 16px and a hairline champagne-gold edge rim (`rgba(246, 231, 176, 0.25)`). Subtle directional shadow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The design system utilizes **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- Standard elements (buttons, scheme tags, input wrappers): `0.5rem` (8px).
- Container cards and dialogue cards: `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px).
- Grand hero spotlight banners: `rounded-xl` at `1.5rem` (24px).

This subtle curve counters cold bureaucratic rigidity without sliding into playful or toy-like aesthetics. For actionable badges denoting verified civic status or voice-assisted prompts, full pill-shapes (`9999px`) are permitted strictly for status capsules to signal interactive tapability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign CTA:** Linear gradient fill from champagne gold (`#F6E7B0`) through burnished gold (`#D4AF37`), with black label text (`#0A0C0E`, font-weight 700). Subtle platinum highlight along top inner border. Provides instant optical salience.
- **Secondary Action:** Obsidian dark ground (`#1A1E24`) bordered by 1px brushed steel (`#CBD5E1`, 30% opacity) with gold/champagne text on hover.
- **Urgent Citizen Alert:** Solid terracotta saffron (`#E07A42`) with crisp ivory text (`#FFFFFF`) for critical deadlines or missing documentation actions.
- **Voice Assist Button:** Floating action pill with gold pulsating perimeter and oversized mic icon for low-literacy voice inquiry.

### Scheme Cards & Entitlement Indicators
- **Card Anatomy:** Dark navy-charcoal surface (`#151B24`) with an ultra-thin 1px metallic rim. Header displays scheme ministry badge, benefit amount in large `Space Grotesk` gold numerals, and a plain-text qualification status badge.
- **Low-Literacy Visual Signals:** Every core requirement features concrete visual iconography (e.g., tractor for farmer subsidies, grain stalk for rations, house for housing schemes) paired with binary status pips: Gilded Checkmark (Eligible) or Neutral Platinum Dash (Pending Verification).

### Form Inputs & Citizen Selectors
- Background is deeply recessed charcoal (`#0E1114`). Borders are matte steel (`#333A44`), focusing to a distinct 2px warm gold glow (`#D4AF37`) without blur.
- Field labels remain permanently visible above the input, supplemented by audio-pronounce icons for accessibility.

### Verification Chips & Filter Tags
- Multi-select criteria filters use pill geometry. Default state is dark steel outline with muted silver text (`#94A3B8`). Selected state fills with dark midnight navy (`#152033`), framed in metallic gold with white text and an active dot pip.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with 4px inner radius (checkbox) or circle (radio). Inactive: 1.5px brushed steel outline. Active: Metallic warm gold background with pitch-black glyph, ensuring absolute contrast.
