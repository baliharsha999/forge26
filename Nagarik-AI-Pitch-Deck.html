Speaker notes

~3.5 min pitch
  1. Hook. Millions of citizens qualify for welfare they never hear about — that gap is the entire pitch. Nagarik AI is a civic copilot that finds the right scheme, explains eligibility and helps apply — in the citizen's own language. Built in 48 hours for the Civic Pulse track. → So what does that broken journey actually look like?
  2. Problem. Today a citizen asks relatives, roams many portals, makes repeat visits — then gives up or pays an intermediary. Five failure points repeat in every scheme: scattered info, legalistic rules, hidden documents, language lock, and no single view of status. Studies keep finding the same thing: awareness and utilization stay low. → So what did we build?
  3. Solution. Four steps in one loop: speak, discover, understand, apply — then we keep following up. Where that used to mean six websites and three visits, it is now one conversation. The side panel lives beside the government portal the whole way. → Here are the six features that make it real.
  4. Features. Voice in Hindi, Telugu or English; 15 source-linked verified schemes; an eligibility matcher; auto-fill that never submits; a three-outcome claim verifier; and escalation drafts up to a Section 6(1) RTI. Every draft is citizen-confirmed, Aadhaar is masked, and API keys never leave the backend. → Next, how the system is put together.
  5. Architecture. The citizen talks to the side panel; the service worker calls FastAPI; RAG over verified scheme data plus live API Setu powers the answer; a content-script adapter fills the portal and captures the tracking ID back into the panel. A second ingestion row keeps the scheme store clean, chunked, embedded and indexed. Fourteen of fourteen contract tests pass. → The proof is in the numbers.
  6. Simplicity + trust. Watch the demo: speak, confirm an editable petition, and the form fills field by field — the citizen always presses submit. Behind it: 14 of 14 tests, 15 verified schemes, 3 languages, and zero auto-submits, ever. That is the trust story. → Finally, where this goes next.
  7. Future + close. Next: more states and districts, more languages with voice-first and IVR, WhatsApp access, deadline reminders, a CSC/NGO operator mode and more portal adapters. Scan the QR to try the live build — the code is open on GitHub. Team: Yugandhar, Harsha and Raghavendra. → Thank you — we're happy to take questions.
